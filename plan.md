# Physics Ball — Project Plan

Option: HTML Game — A physics-based 3D puzzle game.

User: Myself, a college student who enjoys interactive physics challenges.

Build Sentence: When the player charges and launches a ball, they use arrow keys to control its movement and reach a target by navigating ramps and obstacles.

Boundary: The game must not automatically complete the mission. Players must control the ball to reach the goal.

Rejected Idea: A racing game, because I wanted to focus on solving physics puzzles rather than simply completing a race.
