# Dead Road: Ricochet Evolution V5 — Project Plan

**Option:** HTML Game — A physics-based endless zombie survival game.

**User:** College students who enjoy fast-paced action games, survival challenges, and arcade-style gameplay.

**Build Sentence:** When the player launches a ball, they steer along an endless road, destroy approaching zombies and obstacles, collect power-ups, and use special abilities to survive and achieve a high score.

**Boundary:** The game must require player interaction to survive. The player must avoid protected targets, manage survival resources, and cannot remain invincible permanently.

**Rejected Idea:** A racing game, because I wanted to create a game that combines physics-based movement, combat, and survival.

## Project Evolution

**Original Concept — Physics Ball**

The project began as a simple physics puzzle game. The player launched a ball and controlled its movement to reach a target platform.

**Cycle 2 — Zombie Survival**

After reviewing the first version, I decided that the gameplay was too short and repetitive. I changed the concept into an endless survival game featuring approaching zombies, obstacles, and continuous forward movement.

**Additional Development — Versions 3 to 5**

I expanded the game with multiple zombie types, changing environments, WASD controls, speed boosts, collectible items, temporary invincibility, visual upgrades, and Ricochet Rampage.

The final direction focuses on continuous gameplay, satisfying collisions, strategic survival, and visible player progression.

## Design Priorities

- Responsive and understandable controls.
- Continuous gameplay with increasing challenges.
- Strong visual feedback when enemies and obstacles are destroyed.
- Meaningful choices between dangerous and protected targets.
- Power-ups and special abilities that reward continued play.
- A self-contained browser game that can run from a single HTML file.
