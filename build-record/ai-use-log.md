# AI Use Log — Physics Ball

## Cycle 1: Initial Build

**AI Tool:** ChatGPT

**Purpose:** Create the first playable version of Physics Ball.

**My Decisions:**
- I chose a physics-based ball game.
- I selected a third-person camera following the ball.
- I decided to use a charging launch system and arrow-key controls.
- I designed the initial goal around navigating ramps and reaching a target.

**AI Contribution:**
- Helped organize the plan, PRD, and ADRs.
- Generated the initial HTML, CSS, and JavaScript game.

**Result:** The initial game opened and basic controls worked during my first gameplay check.

## Cycle 2: Zombie Survival

**AI Tool:** ChatGPT

**Purpose:** Expand the game into an endless zombie survival experience.

**My Decisions:**
- I wanted the game to continue instead of ending after a short course.
- I decided the ball should maintain forward movement after launching.
- I requested zombies approaching the player.
- I chose collision-based attacks, road obstacles, and continuous survival gameplay.

**AI Contribution:**
- Helped draft ADR-003 and the PRD update.
- Generated the revised zombie survival game.
- Added a survival energy system, scoring, and obstacle behavior.

**Result:** The revised game opened successfully. Formal comparison testing is still pending.

## Limitations

- The zombies use simplified graphics instead of realistic 3D models.
- Physics behavior is simplified.
- The game has not yet been fully verified through four documented tests.

## Disclosure

AI generated the game code and assisted with documentation. I selected the ideas, made the design decisions, and reviewed the playable results.
