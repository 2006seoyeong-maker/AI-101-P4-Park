# ADR-003: Endless Zombie Survival

Status: Accepted (Cycle 2)

Context: The original Physics Ball game had a short course and limited replay value. The player wanted a more engaging experience with continuous movement and survival challenges. This change was motivated by gameplay review, not a documented Cycle 1 test failure.

Decision: Replace the short course with an endless road survival mode. The ball maintains forward movement after launch. Add approaching zombies, collision-based attacks, obstacles, and a survival energy system.

Consequence: The game offers continuous challenges and more replay value, but the increased complexity may introduce new bugs and performance limitations.
